Expense Categorizer & Budget Alert

AI ClassificationGoogle SheetsCode / AggregationIF logicEmail Alert

The Situation

Most people (and small businesses) have no idea where their money goes until the month is over. They jot expenses in a notebook or a messy sheet, but nobody sits down to tag each one as "food" or "transport" — so budgeting never actually happens.

The Pain

  • Categorizing every transaction by hand is boring, so it doesn't get done.
  • By the time you notice you overspent on dining, the money's already gone.
  • No early warning — budgets are reviewed too late to change behaviour.

The Goal

Log an expense in two seconds. The AI tags it with the right category and whether it's a need or a want, saves it to your sheet, and the moment a category crosses its monthly budget, you get an email alert — while you can still do something about it.

What's New Here

AI classification + real aggregation + a threshold alert. This is the first project that reads its own data back: after logging the expense it re-reads the sheet, sums the month's spend in that category with a Code node, and only alerts when a real budget is breached. AI + math + logic together.

The Pipeline

  1. Add Expense (Form): description, amount, date.
  2. AI categorize → category + need/want + merchant + note (Structured Output).
  3. Append the tagged expense to Google Sheets.
  4. Read all expenses back and sum this month's total for that category (Code node).
  5. IF over budget → send a Gmail budget alert.
